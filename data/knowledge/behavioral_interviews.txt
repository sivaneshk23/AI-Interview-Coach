BEHAVIORAL INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

Behavioral interviews evaluate how candidates handle realistic workplace situations.

Common themes include:

- Teamwork
- Leadership
- Conflict resolution
- Failure
- Adaptability
- Time management
- Decision making
- Responsibility
- Learning from mistakes
- Handling pressure
- Collaboration
- Communication

Behavioral questions often use situations from academic projects, internships, work experience, volunteering or other legitimate experiences.

The interviewer should not assume that a candidate has professional experience.

The STAR structure can be used as a response framework:

Situation:
Describe the context.

Task:
Explain the responsibility or objective.

Action:
Explain what the candidate actually did.

Result:
Explain the outcome and learning.

Candidates should avoid inventing experience.

The evaluator should distinguish between:
- quality of the story
- relevance
- ownership
- clarity
- measurable outcome
- reflection and learning

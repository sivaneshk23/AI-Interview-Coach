HR INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

HR interviews commonly explore:

- Tell me about yourself
- Motivation
- Career goals
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Teamwork
- Conflict management
- Adaptability
- Company interest
- Role interest
- Workplace expectations
- Ethical behavior
- Professional communication

Answers should be concise, truthful and relevant.

Candidates should avoid:
- memorized generic answers
- unsupported claims
- excessive personal information
- criticizing previous organizations
- unrealistic salary expectations without context

The interviewer should adapt HR questions to the target role and candidate experience.

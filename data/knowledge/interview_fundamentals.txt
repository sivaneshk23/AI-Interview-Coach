INTERVIEW FUNDAMENTALS

An effective interview evaluates multiple dimensions of a candidate.

Core dimensions include:

1. Technical knowledge
2. Problem-solving ability
3. Communication
4. Relevance to the target role
5. Behavioral maturity
6. Practical experience
7. Analytical thinking
8. Adaptability
9. Confidence without overclaiming
10. Ability to explain concepts clearly

Interview questions should be appropriate for the candidate's experience level.

For fresher candidates:
- Focus on fundamentals.
- Test understanding rather than assuming professional experience.
- Include academic projects and practical learning.
- Avoid requiring experience that the candidate has not claimed.

For experienced candidates:
- Include real-world scenarios.
- Ask about architecture, trade-offs, debugging, ownership and decision-making.
- Explore measurable outcomes from previous work.

A good interview should gradually increase difficulty when the candidate demonstrates strong understanding.

When a candidate gives an incorrect answer, the interviewer should use a constructive follow-up question rather than immediately ending the assessment.

Interview preparation should identify both strengths and gaps.

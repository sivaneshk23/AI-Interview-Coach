APTITUDE INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

Aptitude assessment may include:

- Quantitative reasoning
- Logical reasoning
- Numerical ability
- Percentages
- Ratios
- Averages
- Probability
- Time and work
- Time, speed and distance
- Data interpretation
- Pattern recognition
- Analytical reasoning

Questions should be generated according to the candidate's target role and selected difficulty.

The system should clearly distinguish between:
- question
- expected reasoning
- final answer
- explanation

The evaluator should assess reasoning rather than only the final answer whenever the candidate provides an explanation.

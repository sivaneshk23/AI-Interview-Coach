COMMUNICATION INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

Communication assessment should evaluate:

- Clarity
- Conciseness
- Structure
- Vocabulary
- Professional tone
- Logical flow
- Ability to explain technical concepts
- Listening and response relevance

Technical correctness and communication quality should be evaluated separately.

A technically correct answer can still have poor communication.

A well-spoken answer can still be technically incorrect.

The system should provide actionable feedback rather than simply labeling communication as good or bad.

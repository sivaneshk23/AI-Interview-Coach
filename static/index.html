Welcome

AI Interview Coach is an intelligent interview preparation system that uses IBM Granite foundation models via IBM watsonx.ai to generate adaptive interview questions, evaluate your answers in real time, and provide actionable feedback to help you grow.

  • Adaptive questions driven by your answers and experience level
  • Multi-dimensional evaluation: technical, communication, clarity, and more
  • RAG-enhanced context — knowledge base grounded responses
  • Works for any job role you enter — no fixed role list
  • Personalized improvement recommendations after each answer
  • Full session summary and performance report at the end
All AI generation and evaluation is performed by IBM Granite models running on IBM watsonx.ai. No data leaves the IBM Cloud.
